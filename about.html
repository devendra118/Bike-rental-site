Built around the ride.

The source project describes HariRental Services as a bike-rental business focused on quality equipment, flexible rental packages and support for riders.

02 Expert support — the source describes help with bike selection, route planning and issues.
03 Affordable rates — the source describes competitive pricing and extended-rental/group discounts.
04 Safety — the source says safety gear and regular equipment checks are priorities.
